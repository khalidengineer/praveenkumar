Frequently Asked Questions

Are all diamonds and gemstones GIA/IGI certified?

Yes, every diamond above 0.30 carats arrives with a physical laser-inscribed GIA or IGI Certificate verifying 4Cs: Color, Clarity, Cut, and Carat weight, sealed in tamper-proof atelier packaging.

How does bespoke custom tailoring work for gowns & lehengas?

Once you select "Custom Atelier Fit", our head draper will contact you via HD video call to take 14 precise anatomical measurements. Each piece is hand-tailored with interior corset boning for a seamless glove-like silhouette.

What is your return & exchange policy?

We offer 30-day hassle-free complimentary returns and exchanges on non-customized jewelry and dresses. Items must include their original security tags and certificates.

Is worldwide shipping fully insured?

Yes. Every single parcel is shipped via Brinks or FedEx Armored Priority with 100% full-value transit insurance. Adult signature and photo ID verification are mandatory upon delivery.
